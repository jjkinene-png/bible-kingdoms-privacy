Who we are

Crown & Covenant: Bible Kingdoms (also called Crown & Covenant; Android package com.storycrowndigital.biblekingdoms) is operated by Story Crown Technologies Ltd. This policy describes the current Android app.

Information used by the game

A parent or guardian can create up to four child profiles. A profile can contain a chosen name or nickname and avatar. For age-appropriate quiz play, the app can store a child’s selected age from 4 to 18. The app also stores that profile’s completed levels, current level, quiz scores and attempt counts, earned characters and kingdom rewards, material balance, seen story cards, and audio and narrator preferences. A small tutorial-completed flag is stored for the profile too. The app does not ask for a child’s email address or require an account.

Where information is stored

These profile and game records are stored on the device in Android app-private storage. Each child’s progress and rewards are kept under that child’s profile. The game does not upload profile names, ages, quiz results, progress, or rewards to Story Crown Technologies Ltd., and it does not have an account-sync service. Android backup is enabled, so Android may include app data in device or Google backup according to the device’s backup settings and Google account. A backup could restore local data after reinstalling.

How information is protected

Child profile information is kept in Android app-private storage. Grown-Up Settings and purchase controls are behind the in-app parent challenge. Google Play handles checkout and payment details; the game checks purchase status through Google Play.

Purchases and Google Play

The optional full-game purchase is handled by Google Play. The game asks Google Play for the product and price, opens checkout through Google Play, and checks Google Play ownership when the game connects, resumes, or a parent chooses Restore Purchases. The game receives purchase status and transaction details needed to confirm the purchase. Google Play handles payment details; the game does not receive a parent’s full payment-card details. Purchase ownership belongs to the Google Play account, not to a child profile. The game does not send purchase tokens to Story Crown Technologies Ltd. Google Play processes purchases under its own terms and privacy policy.

Third-party services, network, analytics and ads

The game uses Google Play to check store products and purchase ownership. Spoken narration uses the text-to-speech service installed on the device. Whether that service works offline or sends information to a provider depends on the device and selected speech engine. The game does not use advertising or analytics services and does not show third-party ads. Other than Google Play and device services described in this policy, the game does not send information to an app server. Google and device providers may process technical or transaction information under their own policies. Read Google’s privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Children and parent choices

Crown & Covenant is designed for children and families. Parents or guardians should create and manage profiles, use a nickname rather than sensitive personal details, and use the parent challenge before Grown-Up Settings or purchase controls. The app does not use child profile information for advertising. Parents can review the information shown in profiles and settings on the device.

Retention and deletion

Local profile and game information remains in app storage until it is removed from the device. The current Reset All Progress control resets level completion and the current level; it is not a complete data deletion control and does not remove the child profile or all earned reward records. The app currently has no parent-facing control that erases an entire profile and all data associated with it. To remove local app data, a device owner can clear this app’s storage in Android settings or uninstall the app. Android or Google backups may retain a separate copy according to device settings, so remove any relevant device backup as well if you want it deleted. Google Play keeps purchase records under its own retention and deletion rules; deleting local app data does not delete a Play purchase.

Contact and policy updates

If you email us, we receive your email address and the message you send. Please do not include sensitive information about a child. The policy will be updated if the app’s data practices change.
